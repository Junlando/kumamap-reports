JScan 隱私權政策

Privacy Policy / 개인정보 처리방침

生效及最後更新日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

歡迎使用 JScan（以下簡稱「本 App」）。 本 App 由 Junlando 開發與維護，提供圖片文字辨識與翻譯、 AI 解說、日本商品條碼掃描、商品資訊查詢、匯率換算、部分商品的台灣價格參考、 足跡地圖與旅程相簿，以及其他日本旅遊相關功能。

我們重視您的隱私。本隱私權政策說明本 App 可能蒐集、處理、 使用及保存哪些資料，以及您可以如何管理相關資料。

一、我們可能蒐集或處理的資訊

1. 照片與相機內容

在取得您的授權後，本 App 可能存取裝置的相機或相簿， 讓您拍攝或選取菜單、景點說明、商品包裝、御神籤或其他圖片。

您選擇的圖片可能會傳送至本 App 的伺服器或第三方雲端文字辨識、 翻譯及內容解析服務，以產生辨識、翻譯與解說結果。 本 App 不會在未經您操作或授權的情況下任意讀取相簿中的其他照片。

當您使用「AI 解說」拍照並建立足跡時，該照片會儲存於我們的伺服器， 詳見本節第 6 點「足跡資料」。

2. 圖片中的文字、翻譯及解說內容

本 App 可能處理圖片中辨識出的文字、翻譯結果、AI 解說內容、商品名稱、 商品說明、成分、用途、注意事項及其他解析內容， 以提供翻譯、解說、商品資訊查詢及相關功能。

3. 條碼與商品查詢資訊

當您使用商品掃描功能時，本 App 可能處理您掃描的商品條碼、 查詢關鍵字、商品資料及查詢結果，以提供商品資訊、翻譯、 匯率換算、優惠券推薦及部分商品的台灣價格參考。

4. 位置資訊

在取得您的授權後，本 App 會在您使用「AI 解說」拍照時取得裝置的位置，用於：

  • 讓 AI 解說參考您附近的景點、店家等地點資訊；
  • 在您授權「精確位置」時，將拍照地點記錄為足跡， 顯示於足跡地圖、旅程相簿及個人頁的探索地圖。

本 App 僅在您使用相關功能時取得位置，不會在背景持續追蹤您的位置。 若您僅授權「大致位置」，本 App 不會建立足跡。

從相簿選擇照片使用 AI 解說時，若照片本身含有拍攝位置資訊， 該位置可能會一併傳送以提供解說，但不會建立足跡。

5. 帳號與個人檔案

為了保存及同步您的足跡，本 App 會自動為您建立匿名帳號。 您也可以選擇使用 Google 帳號或 Apple 帳號登入， 此時我們會依您在該服務的設定與授權，取得使用者識別碼、名稱、 電子郵件地址及大頭貼。使用 Apple 登入時，您可以選擇隱藏電子郵件地址。

您在 App 內設定的暱稱及大頭貼會儲存於我們的伺服器， 以便在您登入的其他裝置上顯示。 為管理同一帳號可使用的裝置數量（最多 3 台）， 我們可能記錄已登入裝置的相關資訊。

6. 足跡資料

當您建立足跡時，以下資料會上傳並儲存於我們的伺服器，並與您的帳號連結：

  • 照片及其縮圖；
  • 拍攝位置（經緯度）及定位精度；
  • 拍攝時間及當地時區；
  • AI 解說內容、地點名稱及地點分類；
  • 所在國家及縣市等地區資訊；
  • 旅程分組及您設定的旅程名稱。

足跡資料用於顯示您的足跡地圖、旅程相簿、探索進度， 並在您以同一帳號登入其他裝置時同步顯示。 除非您主動分享，您的足跡不會公開給其他使用者。

7. 使用及分析資料

為了解功能使用情況並改善服務，本 App 可能透過分析工具 （例如 Google Analytics for Firebase）蒐集以下類型的資料：

  • App 開啟、頁面瀏覽及按鈕點擊等互動事件；
  • 翻譯、掃碼、AI 解說、足跡或其他功能的使用情況；
  • 裝置類型、作業系統版本、App 版本及語言設定；
  • 大致地區、連線狀態及其他技術資訊；
  • 匿名或假名化的裝置識別碼及分析識別資訊；
  • 錯誤、當機、效能及診斷資訊。

功能使用事件中可能包含足跡所在的國家或縣市、足跡數量等概略資訊， 但不包含精確位置或照片。 我們不會刻意透過分析事件蒐集您的姓名、電子郵件地址、 照片內容、完整翻譯內容或其他可直接辨識您身分的資訊。

8. 廣告

本 App 透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能蒐集裝置相關資訊、 IP 位址、大致地區及廣告互動資訊，以提供及衡量廣告成效。 本 App 不會要求「App 追蹤透明度」授權， 也不會將您的照片、足跡或精確位置提供給廣告服務。

9. 您主動提供的資訊

當您透過電子郵件、客服、錯誤回報、申請刪除帳號或其他方式聯繫我們時， 我們可能收到您提供的姓名、電子郵件地址、帳號識別碼、問題描述、 截圖、照片、裝置資訊或其他內容。

Welcome to JScan (the “App”), developed and maintained by Junlando. JScan provides image-based text recognition and translation, AI explanations, Japanese product barcode scanning, product information lookup, currency conversion, a footprint map and trip albums, and other travel-related features.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may collect or process, how such information is used and stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We May Collect or Process

1.1 Photos and Camera Content

With your permission, the App may access your device’s camera or photo library so you can capture or select menus, attraction information, product packaging, Omikuji, and other images.

Images selected by you may be transmitted to the App’s servers or third-party cloud-based text recognition, translation, and content processing services to generate recognition, translation, and explanation results. The App does not access other photos in your library without your action or permission.

When you take a photo with AI Scan and it is saved as a footprint, the photo is stored on our servers. See Section 1.6, “Footprint Data.”

1.2 Recognized Text, Translation, and Explanation Content

The App may process text recognized from images, translations, AI explanations, product names, descriptions, ingredients, purposes, warnings, instructions, and other interpreted content to provide the App’s features.

1.3 Barcode and Product Search Information

When you use the product scanning feature, the App may process scanned barcodes, search terms, product data, and search results to provide product information, translations, currency conversion, coupon suggestions, and related features.

1.4 Location Information

With your permission, the App obtains your device’s location when you take a photo with AI Scan, in order to:

  • Help the AI explanation refer to nearby places such as attractions and shops;
  • When you allow Precise Location, record where the photo was taken as a footprint, shown on your footprint map, trip albums, and the exploration map on your profile.

The App obtains your location only while you use these features and does not continuously track your location in the background. If you allow only Approximate Location, the App does not create footprints.

When you select a photo from your library for AI Scan, location information embedded in that photo may be sent to provide the explanation, but no footprint is created.

1.5 Account and Profile

To store and sync your footprints, the App automatically creates an anonymous account for you. You may also choose to sign in with Google or Apple. In that case, depending on your settings and authorization with that service, we receive your user identifier, name, email address, and profile photo. When signing in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address.

The nickname and profile photo you set in the App are stored on our servers so they can be shown on your other signed-in devices. To manage the number of devices that can use the same account (up to 3), we may record information about signed-in devices.

1.6 Footprint Data

When you create a footprint, the following information is uploaded to and stored on our servers and linked to your account:

  • The photo and its thumbnail;
  • The location where the photo was taken (latitude and longitude) and its accuracy;
  • The time the photo was taken and the local time zone;
  • The AI explanation, place name, and place category;
  • Region information such as country and prefecture;
  • Trip grouping and any trip names you set.

Footprint data is used to show your footprint map, trip albums, and exploration progress, and to sync them when you sign in to the same account on another device. Your footprints are not made public to other users unless you choose to share them.

1.7 Usage, Analytics, and Diagnostic Data

To understand how the App is used and to improve our services, the App may use analytics and diagnostic tools, such as Google Analytics for Firebase, to collect information including:

  • App launches, page views, button interactions, and usage events;
  • Use of translation, scanning, AI Scan, footprints, and other features;
  • Device type, operating system version, App version, and language;
  • Approximate region, connection status, and technical information;
  • Pseudonymous device and analytics identifiers;
  • Crash, performance, error, and diagnostic information.

Usage events may include general information such as the country or prefecture of a footprint and the number of footprints, but not precise locations or photos. We do not intentionally include your name, email address, image content, or full translation content in analytics events.

1.8 Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, IP address, approximate region, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not provide your photos, footprints, or precise location to advertising services.

1.9 Information You Provide

If you contact us through email, customer support, error reporting, an account deletion request, or another communication channel, we may receive information you provide, such as your name, email address, account identifier, message, screenshots, photos, device information, or other related content.

We may use information to:

  • Provide image recognition, translation, AI explanations, and content interpretation;
  • Provide barcode scanning, product information lookup, and coupon suggestions;
  • Provide currency conversion and related travel features;
  • Create your account and store and sync your footprints and profile across your devices;

3. Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the App may request:

  • Camera access: Used to capture menus, products, barcodes, attractions, and other content for recognition, scanning, or explanation.
  • Photo library access: Used to select images that you choose for recognition, translation, and explanation, or to save images when you initiate that action.

5. History, Footprints, and Account Data

To allow you to review previous results, the App may store translation history, scanning history, AI Scan history, product information, thumbnails, or related results locally on your device. This local history is not uploaded to our servers for syncing.
