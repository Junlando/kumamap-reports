隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-15

PKing 條碼夾（以下簡稱「本 App」）重視您的隱私。本政策說明本 App 如何處理會員卡、條碼、自訂圖片、iCloud 同步及訂閱等相關資訊。

1. 我們處理的資訊

  • 會員卡資料：您新增或掃描的卡片名稱、卡號、條碼或 QR Code 內容、卡片類型、排序及相關設定。
  • 自訂圖片：您自行選擇的會員卡封面圖片。
  • 相機與相簿：在取得您的授權後，本 App 使用相機掃描條碼，或存取您選取的圖片；您可於 iOS 設定調整相關權限。
  • 裝置與技術資訊：本 App 使用的廣告或技術服務供應商可能處理裝置資訊、App 版本、使用情形及錯誤紀錄，以提供服務及改善穩定性。
  • 廣告資訊：免費版本可能顯示第三方廣告；廣告供應商可能依其政策及您的裝置設定處理廣告識別碼、互動紀錄等資訊。
  • 訂閱資訊：若您訂閱 Premium，交易由 Apple App Store 處理。本 App 可能讀取必要的訂閱狀態或交易識別資訊，以確認功能使用資格；我們不會直接取得完整付款卡片資料。
  • 客服資訊：若您主動聯絡我們，我們會處理您提供的電子郵件內容及必要聯絡資訊，以回覆問題。

2. 資料使用目的

  • 建立、管理、搜尋及顯示會員卡與條碼。
  • 提供桌面小工具、個別會員卡條碼顯示及自訂圖片功能。
  • 透過您的個人 iCloud 帳號同步指定資料，以支援重新安裝或更換裝置後的資料還原。
  • 驗證 Premium 訂閱狀態、提供付費功能及移除廣告。
  • 提供廣告、維護服務穩定性、排除問題及回覆客服需求。

3. 資料儲存

您的會員卡資料（包含卡片名稱、卡號、條碼及自訂圖片）主要儲存於您的裝置本機。我們不會將這些會員卡內容上傳至我們自己的伺服器。為顯示桌面小工具，相關資料可能在您的裝置上由本 App 與其小工具共用。

4. iCloud 資料儲存與備份

若您的裝置已登入 iCloud 並啟用本 App 所需的 iCloud 同步功能，本 App 可將會員卡清單（卡片名稱、卡號及卡片類型）與手機載具條碼儲存於您個人的 iCloud 帳號，以便在更換裝置或重新安裝本 App 時還原資料。這些同步資料由 Apple 的 iCloud 服務管理，我們無法透過自己的伺服器存取。

自訂會員卡圖片不會透過本 App 的上述 iCloud 同步功能儲存，但可能包含在您的 iOS 裝置備份中。iCloud 同步與 iOS 裝置備份是不同機制，資料是否可還原仍取決於您的設定及 Apple 服務狀態。

5. 第三方服務

本 App 可能使用 Apple iCloud（資料同步及裝置備份）、Apple App Store（訂閱與交易處理），以及整合於 App 中的第三方廣告、分析或錯誤回報服務。這些服務可能依其隱私權政策處理提供服務所需的資訊。會員卡內容不會因為本 App 使用廣告服務而主動提供給廣告供應商。

6. 資料分享與揭露

我們不會出售您的會員卡內容，也不會將會員卡名稱、卡號、條碼或自訂圖片上傳至我們的伺服器。為提供訂閱、廣告及必要技術服務，相關供應商可能依各自服務範圍處理必要的技術或交易資訊。如法律要求，我們可能依法提供我們實際持有且依法應提供的資料。

7. 資料保存與刪除

您可以在本 App 內刪除會員卡及自訂內容。移除本 App 後，儲存於裝置本機的相關資料通常會一併刪除，但既有的 iOS 裝置備份不一定會同步移除。

已儲存於 iCloud 的同步資料不一定會因移除本 App 而自動刪除。您可前往 iPhone 的「設定 → Apple 帳號 → iCloud」，查看可用的 App 資料管理選項；實際入口可能因 iOS 版本及資料儲存方式而不同。如需協助，可透過下方電子郵件與我們聯絡。訂閱與交易紀錄的保留及管理依 Apple 規範辦理。

8. 您的選擇與權利

您可透過 iOS 設定管理相機、照片、廣告追蹤及 iCloud 相關權限，並於 Apple 帳號的訂閱管理頁面取消或管理訂閱。依適用法律，您可能有權查詢、存取、更正或刪除我們實際持有的個人資料，以及撤回適用的同意。

9. 資料安全

本機資料的安全性受到裝置本身的安全機制保護；iCloud 資料則由 Apple 提供相關安全保護措施。建議您妥善保管裝置及 Apple 帳號，以保障個人資料安全。

10. 兒童隱私

本 App 並非專為兒童設計，我們不會刻意要求兒童提供個人資料。若您認為有不當處理兒童個人資料的情形，請與我們聯絡。

11. 隱私權政策更新

我們可能因功能、第三方服務或法律要求變更而更新本政策，並於本頁公布最新版本及生效日期。

PKing Wallet

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

PKing Wallet (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles loyalty cards, barcodes, custom images, iCloud synchronization, subscriptions, and related information.

1. Information We Process

  • Loyalty card data: Card names, card numbers, barcode or QR code contents, card types, order, and related settings that you add or scan.
  • Custom images: Loyalty card cover images you choose.
  • Camera and photo library: With your permission, the App uses the camera to scan barcodes or accesses images you select. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Device and technical information: Advertising and technical service providers used by the App may process device information, App version, usage data, and error reports to deliver services and improve reliability.
  • Advertising information: The free version may display third-party ads. Advertising providers may process advertising identifiers and interaction records according to their policies and your device settings.
  • Subscription information: Premium purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. The App may access necessary subscription status or transaction identifiers to verify eligibility. We do not directly receive your full payment card details.
  • Support information: If you contact us, we process your email and information you provide to respond to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create, manage, search, and display loyalty cards and barcodes.
  • Provide home screen widgets, individual card barcode display, and custom image features.
  • Sync specified data through your personal iCloud account to support recovery after reinstalling the App or changing devices.
  • Verify Premium subscriptions, provide paid features, and remove ads.
  • Display ads, maintain reliability, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support requests.

3. Data Storage

Your loyalty card data (including card names, numbers, barcodes, and custom images) is primarily stored locally on your device. We do not upload this loyalty card content to our own servers. To display home screen widgets, the App and its widgets may share relevant data on your device.

4. iCloud Storage and Backups

If your device is signed in to iCloud and the iCloud synchronization features required by the App are enabled, the App may store your loyalty card list (card names, numbers, and types) and mobile e-invoice carrier barcode in your personal iCloud account. This helps restore data when you change devices or reinstall the App. This synchronized data is managed by Apple's iCloud service; we cannot access it through our own servers.

Custom loyalty card images are not stored through the App's iCloud synchronization feature described above, but they may be included in your iOS device backup. iCloud synchronization and iOS device backup are separate mechanisms. Whether data can be restored depends on your settings and the availability of Apple's services.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use Apple iCloud (data synchronization and device backup), the Apple App Store (subscriptions and transactions), and integrated third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services, including Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics. These providers may process information needed to provide their services under their own privacy policies. The App does not intentionally provide loyalty card contents to advertising providers merely because advertising services are used.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your loyalty card content or upload card names, numbers, barcodes, or custom images to our servers. Providers supporting subscriptions, advertising, and essential technical services may process necessary technical or transaction information within the scope of their services. If required by law, we may disclose information that we actually hold and are legally required to provide.

7. Retention and Deletion

You can delete loyalty cards and custom content within the App. Removing the App generally deletes related locally stored data, but existing iOS device backups may remain.

Removing the App does not necessarily delete data already synchronized to iCloud. You can check available App data management options under iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. The exact options may vary by iOS version and storage mechanism. Contact us at the address below if you need assistance. Apple manages retention of subscription and transaction records under its policies.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage camera, photo, ad-tracking, and iCloud permissions in iOS Settings and manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account. Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to inquire about, access, correct, or delete personal information we actually hold, and to withdraw applicable consent.

9. Data Security

Locally stored data benefits from your device's security protections, while Apple provides security measures for iCloud data. We recommend protecting your device and Apple Account to help safeguard your information.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children, and we do not intentionally ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child's personal information has been handled inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in features, third-party services, or legal requirements. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page.
