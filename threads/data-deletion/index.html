Data Deletion Instructions

Junlando Social Listening
Last updated: 11 September 2026

What we hold

Junlando Social Listening stores publicly available Threads posts that match our monitoring keywords. For each post we hold the post identifier, the post text, the author's public username, the public permalink, and the publication timestamp. All such records are deleted automatically 90 days after collection.

How to request deletion

If you are the author of a post and want it removed from our internal store before the automatic 90 day deletion, send an email to info@junlando.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the link to the post.

We will confirm receipt and delete the record within 30 days.

Disconnecting the account we use

The only Threads account connected to this Application is our own. If we disconnect it, the Application stops collecting data and the stored access token is deleted.

Contact

Email: info@junlando.com

資料刪除說明

Junlando Social Listening
最後更新：2026 年 9 月 11 日

我們保存哪些資料

Junlando Social Listening 會儲存符合監測關鍵字的 Threads 公開貼文。每則貼文保存的內容包括：貼文識別碼、貼文內容、作者公開帳號名稱、公開連結，以及發布時間。所有紀錄於蒐集後 90 天自動刪除。

如何申請刪除

若您是貼文作者，希望在 90 天自動刪除前將紀錄移除，請寄信至 info@junlando.com，主旨註明「資料刪除申請」，並附上該則貼文的連結。

我們會回覆確認，並於 30 天內完成刪除。

解除帳號連結

本應用僅連結我方自有的 Threads 帳號。若解除該連結，本應用即停止蒐集資料，所儲存的存取權杖亦會一併刪除。
