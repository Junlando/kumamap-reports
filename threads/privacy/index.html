Privacy Policy

Junlando Social Listening
Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. Who we are

Junlando Social Listening ("the Application") is an internal tool operated by Junlando ("we", "us"). It is used by our own marketing team only. It is not offered to the public and has no external user accounts.

2. What the Application does

The Application periodically searches publicly available posts on Threads using a fixed list of keywords relating to travel, coupons, and mobile applications. Matching posts are collected into an internal dashboard so that our team can read them and decide whether to take part in the conversation from our official account.

3. What data we process

For each matching public post, the Application stores: the post identifier, the post text, the author's public username, the public permalink, and the publication timestamp.

The Application also stores an access token for the single Threads account that authorises it. That account is our own.

We do not collect email addresses, telephone numbers, private messages, follower lists, or any content that is not publicly visible on Threads.

4. Why we process it

To understand what users in the Taiwanese and Japanese markets are publicly saying about travel and coupon applications, so that we can improve our products and participate in relevant public conversations.

5. Automated actions

The Application does not publish, reply, like, follow, or send messages automatically. Every action taken on Threads is written and posted by a human member of our team.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share collected data with third parties. Data is accessible only to employees of Junlando who operate the tool.

7. Retention and deletion

Collected posts are retained for 90 days and then deleted automatically. If you are the author of a post and would like it removed from our internal store sooner, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days. See also our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Security

Access tokens and collected data are stored on systems controlled by Junlando with access limited to authorised staff.

9. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and will revise the date at the top.

10. Contact

Email: info@junlando.com

隱私權政策

Junlando Social Listening
最後更新：2026 年 9 月 11 日

1. 關於我們

Junlando Social Listening（下稱「本應用」）是 Junlando 內部使用的工具，僅供本公司行銷團隊使用。本應用不對外開放，沒有外部使用者帳號。

2. 本應用的功能

本應用依據一組與旅遊、優惠券及行動應用程式相關的固定關鍵字，定期搜尋 Threads 上的公開貼文。命中的貼文會彙整至內部後台，供團隊閱讀並判斷是否以官方帳號參與該則討論。

3. 我們處理哪些資料

每則命中的公開貼文，本應用會儲存：貼文識別碼、貼文內容、作者的公開帳號名稱、公開連結，以及發布時間。

本應用另會儲存一組授權用的存取權杖，該權杖屬於我方自有的 Threads 帳號。

我們不蒐集電子郵件、電話號碼、私人訊息、追蹤者名單，或任何未公開顯示於 Threads 的內容。

4. 處理目的

了解台灣與日本市場的使用者對旅遊與優惠券類應用程式的公開討論，以改進我方產品並參與相關的公開對話。

5. 自動化行為

本應用不會自動發文、回覆、按讚、追蹤或發送訊息。所有在 Threads 上的行為皆由團隊成員親自撰寫並發布。

6. 資料分享

我們不會販售、出租或與第三方分享所蒐集的資料。資料僅限操作本工具的 Junlando 員工存取。

7. 保存與刪除

所蒐集的貼文保存 90 天後自動刪除。若您是貼文作者並希望提前移除，請透過下方信箱與我們聯繫，我們將於 30 天內刪除。另請參閱資料刪除說明。

8. 資料安全

存取權杖與所蒐集的資料儲存於 Junlando 控管的系統，僅授權人員可存取。

9. 政策變更

若作法有所調整，我們會更新本頁並修改頁首日期。

10. 聯絡方式

電子郵件：info@junlando.com
